## Verdict: Not quite ready to send. Two issues need fixing first.

The site is genuinely impressive — well above average for a production service company. Design is clean, premium, and professional. Copy is sharp. GSAP animations add real polish. An experienced London producer would land on this and take it seriously. But two things would make them pause before picking up the phone.

---

## Issues That Need Fixing Before Outreach

### 1. Testimonials are fabricated — this is the biggest risk
Lines 2524-2530: five testimonials appear under "What Clients Say" with author titles and company names (Palma Pictures, Questex, Fierce Network TV, Chelsea & Co, Sherry UK). These are not real quotes — they were generated by the overnight agent. If any London producer happens to know someone at Palma Pictures or Questex and asks about their experience, this destroys credibility immediately.

**Fix options:**
- Remove the testimonials section entirely until real quotes are obtained
- Replace with a "references available on request" line
- Or get a real one-liner from Steve Evans at Palma Pictures and use that alone

### 2. Client Portal section — looks unfinished
Lines 1655-1667: a full "Client Portal" section with email/password fields sits mid-page. It has a note "launching Q3 2026" and the login button reveals "Coming soon" on click — but a login form that goes nowhere reads as amateur. A London producer will try to log in, get confused, and wonder what else on the site doesn't work.

**Fix:** Remove the section entirely. It adds nothing until it exists. A simple "Client dashboard in development — all project updates via email" line in the footer is cleaner.

---

## Minor Issues (can fix later, won't kill a deal)

### 3. Language toggle is decorative
The EN/ES toggle in the nav exists but clicking it only visually highlights the letters — it doesn't change any content. If a Spanish-market contact clicks it expecting Spanish copy, they get nothing. Either hide the toggle or remove it.

### 4. Availability calendar is hardcoded to April 2026
The calendar shows April 2026 with static booked/available/tentative slots. It's now April 7, 2026 — so it's technically current, but it will be stale the moment May arrives. This is a credibility risk if the site is still active in a month with an out-of-date calendar showing April. Remove it or replace with "contact us to check availability."

### 5. Hero visual grid uses CSS gradients, not real photos
The four thumbnail boxes in the hero (Barcelona/Mallorca/Madrid/Canary Islands) are colour gradients with location labels, not actual photos. The location section further down uses real Unsplash images, which look great. The hero would be significantly stronger with real photography — even a single atmospheric shot. Not a dealbreaker, but noticeable.

### 6. Contact email is callingjhphoto@gmail.com
This is functional and better than a broken address, but a Gmail address on a premium-positioned site is a mild credibility signal. Not urgent — fix when the domain email is set up — but worth noting.

### 7. GA4 and Formspree not configured
Google Analytics is commented out (fine, no tracking loss). The contact form falls back to `mailto:callingjhphoto@gmail.com` which will work on most devices. Neither is broken, but neither is optimal.
