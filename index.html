Tax Incentives

Spain pays you to shoot here.

The most competitive incentive framework in Europe. Up to 54% in the Canary Islands.

Spanish National Rebate

30% tax rebate on
qualifying spend

Under Article 36.2 of Spain's Corporate Tax Law, international productions qualify for a 30% rebate on eligible local expenditure. Minimum spend: €1M live-action, €200K animation.

Canary Islands Special Zone

54%

Europe's highest combined rebate through the ZEC special economic zone. Competitive with Caribbean and Middle Eastern incentives.
