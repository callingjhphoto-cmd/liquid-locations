Under Article 36.2 of Spain's Corporate Tax Law, international productions qualify for a 30% rebate on eligible local expenditure. Minimum spend: €1M live-action, €200K animation.

About the Founder

London production.
Spanish ground.

Liquid Locations was founded by James Huertas, a bilingual British-Spanish production professional with over 8 years of experience across London and Spain's most demanding shoots.

Having worked with companies like Palma Pictures and across London's commercial and fashion production scene, James identified a critical gap: international producers forced to choose between expensive, inflexible conglomerates and local fixers who did not understand London standards.

Liquid Locations sits precisely in that gap — delivering the logistical muscle and production value of an established service company, with the agility, transparency, and cultural fluency of a modern boutique.
