Tax Incentives

Spain pays you to shoot here.

The most competitive incentive framework in Europe — up to 54% in the Canary Islands. For film, series, animation and documentary. Advertising commercials and stills campaigns do not qualify — see below.

Spanish Production Rebates

Mainland Spain 30%
Canary Islands (ZEC) 54%

Under Article 36.2 of Spain's Corporate Tax Law (Ley 27/2014), international productions qualify for a 30% rebate on eligible local expenditure. Minimum spend: €1M live-action, €200K VFX and post.

Who qualifies. Article 36.2 covers feature films and audiovisual fiction, animation and documentary series. Advertising commercials (spots publicitarios) and stills photography are excluded — Ley 27/2014 Art. 36.2, confirmed by AEAT binding rulings V1746-15 and V2300-21. If you are shooting a commercial or a stills campaign, budget without the rebate. Our saving comes from the production fee, not from Hacienda.

Canary Islands Special Zone

Europe's highest combined rebate through the ZEC special economic zone. Competitive with Caribbean and Middle Eastern incentives.
