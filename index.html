Tax Incentives

Spain's production incentives are among Europe's strongest

The Spanish government's "Spain, Audiovisual Hub of Europe" plan backs over €1.6 billion in public investment for 2021–2025. Here is what your production can access.

30%
Tax deduction on first €1M spend

Corporation Tax deduction for international productions shooting on mainland Spain. 25% on expenditure above €1M.

54%
Canary Islands rebate

The Canary Islands offer one of Europe's most generous production rebates, making Tenerife and Gran Canaria exceptional value for large-scale shoots.

SPV
We handle the structure

International productions must partner with a registered Spanish service company or establish a local SPV. Liquid Locations manages this process end-to-end.

About

Eight years in production. Two countries. One standard.

Liquid Locations was founded by James Huertas, a freelance digital technician and production professional who spent seven years working across London's commercial photography and advertising production industry before relocating to Spain.

Based between Barcelona and Mallorca, James works regularly with Palma Pictures and other leading Spanish service companies, while maintaining deep relationships across the London production landscape. This dual perspective enables Liquid Locations to deliver a seamless bridge between London's creative ambition and Spain's world-class production infrastructure.

Whether it is a two-day stills campaign for a premium spirits brand or a week-long TVC for a global automotive client, Liquid Locations brings London precision to every Spanish shoot.
