## Notes on Usage

- **Personalise heavily** — reference a specific recent project the company shot, or a specific photographer on the agent's roster
- **Attach the location library link** — this is the lead magnet; it demonstrates capability immediately
- **Follow up** — WhatsApp or phone within 48 hours of sending. Production people live on WhatsApp, not email.
- **Timing** — send Tuesday–Thursday, 9:00–11:00 AM London time. Avoid Mondays (shoot prep) and Fridays (wrap days).
- **Subject line** — keep it specific and benefit-led. Avoid generic "introduction" language.
