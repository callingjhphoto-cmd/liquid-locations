## Pricing by Client Market

### London / International Clients
- Full premium rates (20% management fee)
- Offset by inherent savings of shooting in Spain vs. UK
- Average 3-day commercial local spend: €52,500–€87,000

### Spanish Domestic Clients
- Compressed markups (10–15%) to compete with local fixers
- Day rates remain standard (APCP alignment)

## What's Included at Each Tier

### Tier 1 — Scout (€450/day)
- Creative brief translation
- Proprietary digital location library access
- Bespoke physical scouting
- GPS-tagged, 360-degree location packages
- Municipal permit acquisition
- On-set location management

### Tier 2 — Coordinate (+15–20% markup)
- Everything in Tier 1
- Local bilingual crew procurement
- Equipment rental coordination
- Transport and logistics planning
- Full line-by-line cost transparency

### Tier 3 — Full Service (+20% of BTL)
- Everything in Tier 2
- End-to-end production management
- High-end catering coordination
- Accommodation and travel logistics
- Comprehensive civil liability insurance
- Single point of contact, start to wrap
